What Pyrae does

🛰️

Satellite detection

NASA FIRMS VIIRS satellite data detects thermal anomalies from space on every overpass — catching fires from orbit.

📸

Citizen reports

Anyone on the ground can upload a photo. An AI model screens every report for visible fire and smoke before it reaches the map.

📈

Predictive spread

A Bayesian Fire Grid estimates where each fire is likely to spread, factoring in wind, terrain, and fuel conditions.

🛣️

Road risk

Roads near active fires are assessed for time-to-arrival and graded by risk tier, using real wind data.

Who it's for

🏘️

Communities

Residents near fire-prone areas get early awareness of active fires and predicted spread — before the smoke reaches them.

🚒

Emergency responders

A real-time common operating picture: confirmed fires, satellite hotspots, and spread forecasts in a single view.

🏛️

Agencies & utilities

REST and CAP APIs let agencies integrate Pyrae data into existing workflows — no system replacement required.

Report a fire you can see

If you see smoke or flames, open the report panel on the map, attach a photo and your location. The AI screen runs instantly and the report is added to the Fire Grid, corroborated against satellite data where available.
