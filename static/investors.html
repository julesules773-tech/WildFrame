Pyrae — wildfire early detection

We're building an AI-powered wildfire early detection network that catches fires before they become large-scale disasters — combining citizen photo reports, live satellite data and predictive spread modelling into a single intelligence platform.

The problem

🔥

Wildfires are getting worse

Climate change is lengthening fire seasons, expanding the wildland-urban interface, and increasing the frequency and intensity of fire starts. The global cost of wildfires exceeds $50 billion annually — and the trend is accelerating.

⏱️

Existing detection is slow

Current detection methods — satellite passes every 3–12 hours, lookout towers, routine patrols — leave a dangerous gap between ignition and first alert. By the time a fire is detected, it may already be too large to contain efficiently.

💰

Suppression costs spiral

Every hour of delayed detection exponentially increases suppression costs and property loss. Early detection — catching fires when they're still small — is the single highest-leverage investment in wildfire resilience.

Our approach

📸

Citizen-powered detection

Anyone with a phone can report a suspected fire with a photo and GPS location. This creates a ground-truth detection network that complements satellite coverage — catching fires that space-based sensors miss, faster than any automated system alone.

🧠

AI-verified reports

Every photo is screened by a computer-vision model for visible fire and smoke. Only reports corroborated by satellite data or nearby independent sightings reach the confirmed layer — eliminating false alarms automatically.

🛰️

Satellite corroboration

Live NASA FIRMS hotspot data validates ground reports from space. A photo sighting within 3 km of a satellite detection in the past 12 hours turns a single observation into a confirmed incident — without waiting for the next satellite pass.

📈

Predictive spread modelling

A Bayesian fire grid models how each fire is likely to evolve — factoring in wind, terrain, vegetation and humidity. Communities and responders get a probabilistic view of where the fire will be in the coming hours, not just where it is now.

Market opportunity

  • Global wildfire management market projected to reach $10+ billion by 2030, driven by increasing fire frequency and stricter regulations.
  • Emergency services modernisation — agencies worldwide are investing in real-time intelligence platforms to replace legacy detection and response workflows.
  • Insurance & utility sector demand — insurers need verified fire timelines for claims; utilities need real-time risk data for preemptive shutoffs and infrastructure protection.
  • Wildland-urban interface expansion — more communities are being built in fire-prone areas, creating demand for early warning systems at the community level.

Traction

🚀

Beta launched

The platform is live and operational. Citizens can report fires, the AI pipeline screens reports in real time, satellite corroboration is active, and predictive spread modelling is running on confirmed incidents.

✅

Validated detection pipeline

Our AI ensemble (YOLOv26 + EfficientNet-B0) achieves 100% recall on fire detection with 99.7% F1 — validated on a real-world dataset of 755 fire and 244 clean images. The corroboration gate ensures false positives never auto-approve.

🗺️

Live map & API

A public live map demonstrates the system in action. A REST API enables integration with existing CAD, GIS and alerting infrastructure — ready for agency pilots.
