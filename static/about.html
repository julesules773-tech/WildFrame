ABOUT PYRAE

Who we are

Pyrae is building an early wildfire detection network that combines citizen photo reports, AI analysis, live satellite data, and predictive spread modelling — so small ignitions are caught before they become large-scale disasters.

Our mission

⏱️

Earlier detection

Every minute counts. By fusing what people on the ground see with what satellites observe from space, we aim to flag fires sooner than any single source could alone.

🌍

Everyone can help

A wildfire can start anywhere — so the detection network is open to anyone. One photo from a phone can be the first signal of a fire that satellites haven't seen yet.

🤝

Built for responders

Our forecasts — where a fire will spread, which roads are at risk — are designed to give communities, utilities, and emergency agencies actionable intelligence, not just alerts.

How it works

📸

1 · Citizen reports

Anyone can report a suspected fire with a photo and location straight from the map.

🧠

2 · AI screening

A computer-vision model checks every photo for visible fire and smoke, with per-class confidence scores.

🛰️

3 · Satellite corroboration

Live NASA FIRMS hotspot data validates reports from space — turning single sightings into confirmed incidents.

📈

4 · Predictive modelling

A Bayesian model estimates where each fire is likely to spread, which roads are at risk, and how the fire may evolve with the weather.

What we stand for

  • Open participation — detection shouldn't depend on expensive infrastructure; a phone camera is enough to contribute.
  • Data-driven decisions — every fire on the map is backed by evidence: photos, satellite detections, or both.
  • Transparency — we show our confidence and our sources, so users can judge for themselves.
  • Safety first — this is an early-warning tool, not a substitute for official emergency services.
