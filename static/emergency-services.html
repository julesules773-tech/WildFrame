Wildfire early detection for emergency services

Catch small ignitions before they become large-scale disasters. Pyrae fuses citizen photo reports, AI screening, live satellite data and predictive spread modelling into a single operational map — giving civil protection, municipalities, utilities and insurers the earliest possible warning.

The challenge

⏱️

Minutes matter

Wildfires double in size every 30–60 minutes under dry, windy conditions. Traditional detection — satellite passes every 3–12 hours, lookout towers, routine patrols — often leaves a dangerous gap between ignition and first alert.

🌍

Scale is overwhelming

Climate change is lengthening fire seasons and expanding the wildland-urban interface. Agencies face more fire starts, wider frontiers, and shrinking response windows — all with the same or fewer resources.

📡

Data is fragmented

Satellite feeds, citizen reports, weather models and road networks live in separate systems. Synthesising them in real time — under pressure, during an incident — is a manual, error-prone process.

What Pyrae delivers

🗺️

Real-time fire map

A single operational map shows confirmed fires, pending reports, satellite hotspots and predicted spread — updated every 8 seconds. No toggling between tools; everything your team needs is in one view.

🧠

AI screening

Every citizen photo is screened by a computer-vision model for visible fire and smoke, with per-class confidence scores. Only corroborated reports reach the confirmed layer — false alarms are filtered automatically.

🛰️

Satellite corroboration

Live NASA FIRMS hotspot data validates ground reports from space. A photo sighting within 3 km of a satellite detection in the past 12 hours is confirmed — turning single observations into reliable incidents.

📈

Spread forecasts

A Bayesian fire grid models how each fire is likely to evolve, factoring in wind, terrain, vegetation and humidity. Get a probabilistic view of where the fire front will be in the next hours — not a static snapshot.

🚗

Road risk assessment

Pyrae identifies which evacuation routes and access roads are threatened by the predicted fire spread — so incident commanders can pre-position resources and advise communities before roads are cut off.

🔌

REST & CAP API

Integrate Pyrae data into your existing CAD, GIS or alert infrastructure via a simple REST API. CAP (Common Alerting Protocol) output is available for interoperability with public warning systems.

Use cases

🚒

Civil protection

Get the earliest possible alert on new fire starts, with predicted spread and road-risk intelligence to guide deployment decisions before the fire reaches critical size.

⚡

Utility shutoffs

Monitor fire risk near critical infrastructure — transmission lines, substations, pipelines — and trigger preemptive shutoffs or inspections based on real-time proximity and spread forecasts.

🛡️

Insurance claims

Establish a verified timeline and geolocation for each fire event: first detection, satellite confirmation, spread trajectory and affected area — reducing claims adjudication time and disputes.

Trusted data sources

  • NASA FIRMS — active fire detections from MODIS and VIIRS satellites, refreshed every 3 hours.
  • EFFIS / Copernicus — European Forest Fire Information System for fire weather indices and historical context.
  • Open-Meteo — real-time wind, temperature and humidity for spread modelling.
  • Citizen photo reports — ground-truth sightings screened by AI, validated against satellite data.
  • OpenStreetMap — road network data for evacuation route risk assessment.

Important disclaimer

Pyrae is an early-warning and intelligence tool — not an emergency service. It does not dispatch firefighters, issue evacuation orders or replace official channels. All confirmed incidents should be verified through your agency's standard operational procedures before action is taken.
