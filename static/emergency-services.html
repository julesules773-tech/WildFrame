FOR AGENCIES & RESPONDERS

Wildfire early detection for emergency services

Catch small ignitions before they become large-scale disasters. Pyrae fuses citizen photo reports, AI screening, live satellite data and predictive spread modelling into a single operational map.

The challenge

⏱️

Minutes matter

Wildfires double in size every 30–60 minutes under dry, windy conditions. Traditional detection leaves a dangerous gap between ignition and first alert.

🌍

Scale is overwhelming

Climate change is lengthening fire seasons and expanding the wildland-urban interface. Agencies face more fire starts, wider frontiers, and shrinking response windows.

📡

Data is fragmented

Satellite feeds, citizen reports, weather models and road networks live in separate systems. Synthesising them in real time is a manual, error-prone process.

What Pyrae delivers

🗺️

Real-time fire map

A single operational map shows confirmed fires, pending reports, satellite hotspots and predicted spread — updated every 8 seconds.

🧠

AI screening

Every citizen photo is screened by a computer-vision model for visible fire and smoke, with per-class confidence scores.

🛰️

Satellite corroboration

Live NASA FIRMS hotspot data validates ground reports from space.

📈

Spread forecasts

A Bayesian fire grid models how each fire is likely to evolve, factoring in wind, terrain, vegetation and humidity.

🚗

Road risk assessment

Pyrae identifies which evacuation routes and access roads are threatened by the predicted fire spread.

🔌

REST & CAP API

Integrate Pyrae data into your existing CAD, GIS or alert infrastructure via a simple REST API.

Use cases

🚒

Civil protection

Get the earliest possible alert on new fire starts, with predicted spread and road-risk intelligence to guide deployment decisions.

⚡

Utility shutoffs

Monitor fire risk near critical infrastructure and trigger preemptive shutoffs or inspections based on real-time proximity and spread forecasts.

🛡️

Insurance claims

Establish a verified timeline and geolocation for each fire event, reducing claims adjudication time and disputes.

Trusted data sources

  • NASA FIRMS — active fire detections from MODIS and VIIRS satellites, refreshed every 3 hours.
  • EFFIS / Copernicus — European Forest Fire Information System for fire weather indices.
  • Open-Meteo — real-time wind, temperature and humidity for spread modelling.
  • Citizen photo reports — ground-truth sightings screened by AI, validated against satellite data.
  • OpenStreetMap — road network data for evacuation route risk assessment.

Important disclaimer

Pyrae is an early-warning and intelligence tool — not an emergency service. It does not dispatch firefighters, issue evacuation orders or replace official channels.
