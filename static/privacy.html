PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: [Date]. This policy explains what Pyrae collects, why we collect it, and how it is stored, shared, and protected.

1. What we collect

📸

Photos

Images you upload when reporting a possible fire. Photos are used to verify and screen reports, and confirmed reports may appear publicly on the map.

📍

Location

GPS coordinates of your report (from your device or the photo's metadata). We use them to place the fire on the map and model its spread.

🕒

Time & device data

When the photo was taken, your device's reported heading (if available), and an anonymous session identifier used to group your uploads.

2. How we use your data

  • Fire detection & mapping — to place reports on the live map and confirm incidents.
  • AI screening — photos are analysed by a computer-vision model to detect visible fire and smoke.
  • Satellite corroboration — report locations are cross-checked against NASA FIRMS satellite hotspot data.
  • Risk modelling — locations and timestamps feed our Bayesian spread and road-risk estimates.

3. Where data is stored & how long

☁️

Storage

Photos are stored in Amazon S3 (us-east-1). Reports and map data are stored in a managed PostgreSQL database (Neon, us-east-1).

⏳

Retention

Reports remain visible on the public map for 48 hours. Records are kept longer for safety, moderation, and verification purposes; rejected reports and their photos are deleted.

🔐

Access

All traffic is served over HTTPS. Moderator actions (accept/reject) are protected by an access-controlled admin interface.

4. Sharing

  • Public map — confirmed incidents (including the photo and location) are shown publicly to help others.
  • Service providers — we share limited data with the processors that power the service: Roboflow (AI photo analysis), NASA FIRMS (satellite data), Open-Meteo (weather), and EFFIS / Copernicus Emergency Management Service (fire-weather indices).
  • No sale — we do not sell your personal data.
  • Legal — we may disclose data where required by law or to protect safety.
