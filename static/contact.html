What Pyrae does

📸

AI photo analysis

Every citizen report is screened for visible fire and smoke by a computer-vision model, with per-class confidence scores.

🛰️

Satellite corroboration

Live NASA FIRMS hotspot data validates reported fires from space, turning single sightings into confirmed incidents.

🔥

Predictive spread grid

A Bayesian model estimates where each fire is likely to spread — and how likely any given area is to burn.

🛣️

Road risk assessment

Roads near an active fire are assessed for time-to-arrival and graded by risk tier, using real wind data.

🏛️

Agency-ready data

Machine-readable alert feeds (CAP / REST API) can be integrated so official incident data flows straight onto the map.

🌬️

Weather-aware

Real wind speed and direction drive spread estimates, smoke drift, and risk scoring.

Who we're looking for

For investors

Pyrae is an early-stage wildfire detection platform focused on detecting fires earlier and giving communities, utilities, and emergency agencies actionable intelligence before small ignitions become large-scale disasters. Wildfires are growing in frequency and intensity, and communities, utilities, and agencies all need faster, cheaper detection.

  • Market opportunity \u2014 who needs this and why now.
    The initial market is organizations with high exposure to wildfire risk\u2014including utilities, municipalities, forestry operators, and emergency-response agencies\u2014that need earlier detection to reduce damage, response costs, and operational risk. Climate-driven fire risk is increasing while existing detection infrastructure can be expensive, slow, or difficult to deploy at scale.
  • Traction \u2014 pilots, users, data, or deployments so far.
    Beta platform launched. Early validation is focused on testing heatmap creation in live situations.
  • What you're building toward and what the round funds
    Pyrae is building toward a scalable, continuously operating wildfire detection network. This round will fund: infrastructure & reliability, compliance & production services, human operations, model & algorithm quality, and traction & pilots

For government agencies

Pyrae gives civil protection, forestry, and emergency-response agencies a real-time common operating picture for wildfire incidents, combining official data, satellite observations, and public reports to help teams detect fires earlier, assess risk, and coordinate response.

  • Real-time incident map \u2014 confirmed and suspected fires in a single operational view
  • AI-screened citizen reports \u2014 crowdsourced reports filtered for relevance and cross-checked against satellite data
  • Spread and road-risk forecasts \u2014 support evacuation planning, access decisions, and resource deployment
  • Direct system integration \u2014 REST/CAP APIs for ingesting Pyrae alerts and intelligence into existing workflows

The result is a faster, shared view of emerging incidents and their potential impact\u2014without requiring agencies to replace their existing systems.
